# Pricing — Onramp

Every plan includes the whole product. Plans differ only by team size and volume.

Included on every plan: live architecture map, Ask (answered from your code), learning paths with quizzes, first issues sized for a new hire, task and review queue, ramp dashboard for leads, Slack / Linear / Jira / GitLab integrations, tenant-isolated data with encrypted tokens.

## Free
- Price: ₹0
- Limits: 1 member, 1 repository, 50 AI credits per month
- Support: community

## Startup
- Price: ₹999/month, or ₹9,999/year (2 months free)
- Limits: up to 5 members, 10 repositories, 5,000 AI credits per month
- Support: email

## Professional
- Price: ₹2,999/month, or ₹29,999/year (2 months free)
- Limits: up to 20 members, 50 repositories, 50,000 AI credits per month
- Support: priority
- 14-day trial on your first subscription

## Enterprise
- Price: custom, via https://developer-onboard.vercel.app/contact
- Limits: unlimited members, repositories and credits
- Dedicated support; SSO and SLA scoped with you

---

*All prices in INR, billed through Razorpay. Cancel any time; access runs to the end of the billing period.*
*Onramp is not SOC 2 certified.*
